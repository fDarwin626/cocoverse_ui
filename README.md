# cocoverse_ui

Animated 3D and particle effects for Flutter. CocoVerse UI is a free package of ready-made widgets: spheres, rings, characters, loaders and full-screen backdrops. Each effect is one widget that you place in your app.

**See it live on our Vercel app:** https://REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-VERCEL-LINK

## Install

```
flutter pub add cocoverse_ui
```

## Effects

- **Blossom**: a lotus cast in pink resin and lit from inside, that opens from a bud.
- **Capsule sprite**: a sprite with pill eyes sealed in a glass capsule, watching your finger.
- **Clay bot**: a small clay robot with a screen for a face that follows your pointer, hops and waves.
- **Crossfold**: three bars that fold out of a stack into a cross, as a loader in metal or plastic.
- **Cube sphere**: thousands of small glossy cubes on a sphere that tumble and breathe in a wave.
- **Fireworks**: a firework show with an optional animated title and subtitle.
- **Foil ring**: a twisting ring covered in wavy foil brush strokes.
- **Gaze**: a wet, glossy eyeball that follows your finger or cursor.
- **GeoMorph**: a solid that morphs between a box, a sphere, an octahedron and a torus.
- **Glass ring**: a turning ring of clear glass that bends the text behind it.
- **Glossy sphere**: a glossy sphere with flowing bands of colour and a bright rim.
- **Halo**: rings turning inside each other like a gyroscope, as a loader or progress ring.
- **Hourglass**: an hourglass of glowing gradient rings, made to sit behind your app.
- **Infiloop**: glowing fibres twisting round an infinity loop, which can also be a loader.
- **Jelly sphere**: a glossy sphere that squashes like jelly when you press it.
- **Link chain**: interlocked links hanging on rope physics that you can tilt, shake and grab.
- **Meteor**: a cracked meteor with glowing seams, hanging in a swarm of shapes and rock chips.
- **Mito**: a hovering robot on three rings of light that glides to follow your pointer.
- **Moonbloom**: a night scene of a blossoming tree with petals drifting down.
- **Particle cube**: a cube built from particles.
- **Pisces**: a goldfish that swims on its own and follows your finger.
- **Plush**: a plush, furry effect with a progress mode and an optional widget in the middle.
- **Shard sphere**: a sphere of prism-shaped shards that grow and light up near your finger.
- **Starfield sphere**: a clear glass sphere with small stars drifting inside it.
- **Timeline**: photos that flip over tile by tile, like a split-flap board.
- **Urchin**: a glass-bright sea urchin with spikes that slowly morph.
